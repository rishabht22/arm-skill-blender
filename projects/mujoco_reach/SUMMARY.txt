MuJoCo reach and lift

The first scene put a UR5e and a Jaco on opposite sides of a handled container.
The motion preview drives the arms up and down. The later reaching demo has each
arm move to a point 3 cm above its handle and try to hold there for three seconds.

Robosuite TwoArmLift supplies the simulator state, rewards, and randomized resets.
There was no external recording dataset. The first PPO pilot observed both arms
and drove them jointly. The later version trained one state-based policy per arm
with the partner idle, using a 128-by-128 MLP and saved observation normalization.
The combined evaluator loads both policies and measures their simultaneous hold.

The joint lift pilot struggled. Reaching isolates the approach before grasping
and lifting. Show experiments.basic_robot_motion first, then compare per-arm and
combined evaluation using the commands in README.md.
