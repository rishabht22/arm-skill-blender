WEAVE native GPU audit and cell

WEAVE moves the coordination experiments into a native RoboFactory two-Panda
scene on the lab GPU. The cell includes a hinged lid, randomized objects and
resets, contact checks, and per-environment rollout monitoring.

Phase 0 verified the five published RoboFactory task archives and sampled
training episodes only. It replayed absolute and normalized joint commands
and measured simulator throughput and contact behavior at several batch
sizes. Every task missed the behavior-cloning precheck. The GPU benchmarks
and contact probes supported the native backend choice.

Phase 1 adds the shared cell and a random-action integrity smoke test. The
next gate requires a supported NVIDIA Linux host. There is no trained WEAVE
policy yet. Show the historical replay and throughput results in
results/phase0/iconlab/report.md.
