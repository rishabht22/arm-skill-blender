CALVIN frozen-skill composition

A single Panda in the CALVIN-D play scene carries out language-conditioned
skills such as opening a drawer and moving blocks. A high-level selector calls
one skill at a time in a continuous scene. The drawer and objects stay where
the last skill left them.

The official HULC_D_D checkpoint, CALVIN scene and normalization metadata, and
released MiniLM language embeddings come from the CALVIN and HULC authors.
Inference uses RGB and robot state. The selector sees privileged simulator
progress and a supplied goal graph. It warms up on synthetic progress states
from the training recipes, then uses PPO over live frozen-HULC rollouts. A
scripted selector qualifies tasks before optimization.

Twelve of the released instructions are active in this experiment. Train and
test recipes are separated in experiments/configs/composition_tasks.json.
Some chained skills fail when the second skill starts from the state left by
the first. The clip in outputs/demo/preview.mp4 shows the native scene on a
successful single-skill LED rollout.
