Goal-conditioned workbench and tower

Ask the arms to move a block to a goal. When the picking arm can reach the
goal it can place directly. When reach is split, the block can pass through a
shared dock. The tower adds three colored blocks, support relations, and a
layout where the shared dock starts occupied.

The single-block data came from 150 published LongPipelineDelivery episodes.
Gripper and joint commands supply timestamps, and source task programs guide
goal and reach annotations. The tower adds ThreeRobotsStackCube for 300 source
episodes and 1,500 decision rows. Supervised scoring learns which legal call
to make. MuJoCo measures state and runs the pick, place, and stack controllers.

The learned single-block controller succeeded on all 20 trials in the saved
layout comparison. The tower's greedy learned controller failed all 25 trials.
Guided search and plain search each succeeded on all 25. The guided
occupied-dock demo shows how the extra staging constraint changes the plan.
