Planar pushing and lifting dispatch

Two mobile robots collect yellow cubes into a red corner receptacle. The
pusher and lifter use separate released Spatial Intention Maps checkpoints.
Each network scores actions on its own overhead observation map. The PyBullet
simulator executes the chosen action before the next dispatch.

The source simulator and weights are downloaded at pinned revisions and verified
by hash. Training data for the 1,764-parameter head came from live simulator
episodes. PPO learned to choose push, lift, or both, with reward for delivery
and distance progress and costs for elapsed simulation time and decisions.

The one-cube demo took five dispatches and 4.57 simulated seconds. In the
five-seed two-cube pilot, lifting alone succeeded three times and the learned
head succeeded zero times. Watch the robots interfere when dispatched together.
The one-cube recording is in outputs/demo/preview.mp4.
