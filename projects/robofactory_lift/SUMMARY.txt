RoboFactory LiftBarrier

Two Panda arms face a shared bar. Each arm can approach and grasp its own
handle, but a level lift requires both ends to rise together.

The training source was 150 published RoboFactory LiftBarrier demonstrations,
pinned to dataset revision 63f8a85. Gripper commands and changes in joint targets
mark approach, grasp, lift, and hold boundaries. Whole episodes are split
105/22/23. A two-layer MLP is trained with weighted behavior cloning on current
joint position, velocity, and executor completion signals. The MuJoCo demo uses
fixed joint-target scripts calibrated from training-episode endpoints.

The head matched 95.1% of held-out joint decisions. A fixed sequence matched
100%. With one arm delayed, waiting for both grasps before lifting let both
guarded controllers succeed. outputs/demo/preview.mp4 shows the comparison.
