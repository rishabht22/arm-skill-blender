RoboFactory multi-task cell

Two Panda arms work across a shared table. One stages parts for the other,
which stacks them or places them inside a container after the lid opens.
The longer evaluation graphs contain up to 17 operations.

The data are 750 original published RoboFactory episodes across PickMeat,
StackCube, PassShoe, PlaceFood, and TwoRobotsStackCube. Task programs and
recorded joint and gripper commands supply weak operation labels. A shared
candidate scorer learns dispatch and WAIT by categorical behavior cloning.
Goal dependencies and arm roles are supplied. Scripted IK controllers and
proximity-gated grasp welds execute the skills in the MuJoCo proxy.

On 20 three-part relay-stack seeds, the learned head succeeded physically on
all seeds and averaged 23.58 simulated seconds. Serial averaged 33.11 seconds
and greedy averaged 22.89. The stricter collision-and-dispatch criterion was
met on 80% of learned trials. See outputs/demo/preview.mp4 for a relay.
