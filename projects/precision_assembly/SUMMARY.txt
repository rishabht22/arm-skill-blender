Precision assembly scheduling

Two Panda stations share one MuJoCo simulation. Each kit visits both stations
for rod or peg insertion, gear seating, connector mating, and final assembly.
Multiple kits arrive at different times. The scheduler decides which ready
job each station starts.

The pinned AssembleNIST release supplies a frozen, multi-task joint-action
expert, workpiece meshes, and the native contact plugin. The two arms have
disjoint catalogs covering 12 variants. Physical qualification measured skill
durations and passed 59 of 60 trials. A duration surrogate uses those
measurements to generate randomized work orders. The head learns from
lookahead scheduling labels, then DAgger adds labels at states it visits.

In the original three-batch physical pilot, the learned head averaged 35.57
simulated seconds versus 54.56 for serial and 34.36 for a Johnson-style
heuristic. Show two jobs running in parallel, then compare all schedulers using
the same work orders.
